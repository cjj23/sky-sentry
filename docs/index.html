Eyes Out

ADS-B traffic at a glance.

What you need

A uAvionix SkyEcho 2, or another receiver that broadcasts GDL90 over Wi-Fi. Eyes Out does not receive traffic on its own and does not work without one.

Getting started

  1. Power on your SkyEcho and let it get a GPS fix.
  2. In iPhone Settings, join the SkyEcho's Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Eyes Out. Traffic appears within seconds.

With no receiver to hand, you can try the app on the ground using the test feed: tap No receiver? Try the test feed on the main screen. The aircraft it shows are invented and tell you nothing about real airspace.

Seeing it beside your moving map on iPad

iPadOS 26 folded Split View and Slide Over into its windowing system, so you need Windowed Apps or Stage Manager to see two apps at once. Open Settings › Multitasking & Gestures and choose Windowed Apps, then drag inward from a corner of Eyes Out to shrink it into a window.

Eyes Out is an aid to lookout. It is not a collision avoidance system, not a navigation system, and it is not TCAS. It will never tell you to climb, descend or turn.

It shows only the aircraft your receiver reports to it. Many aircraft cannot be seen by it at all, including those with no ADS-B Out — which is most of the UK general aviation fleet — gliders and others using FLARM, and anything with its transponder switched off. A green screen does not mean the airspace around you is empty.
