Privacy Policy — Eyes Out

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Eyes Out does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

No accounts, no analytics

The app has no sign-in, no user accounts and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not operate a server and the app sends no data to us.

Traffic data

Aircraft positions are received directly from your own ADS-B receiver over its local Wi-Fi network. This data is processed on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location as a backup source of your own aircraft's position when your receiver has no GPS fix, and to continue receiving traffic while another app is in the foreground. Your location is used on the device only. It is never transmitted, stored off the device, or shared. You may refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app continues to work using the position reported by your receiver.

Flight recordings

If you choose to record a flight, the recording is written to storage on your device and stays there. Nothing is uploaded. You control whether recordings are shared, using the standard iOS share sheet, and you can delete them at any time from within the app or the Files app.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information from Apple about subscribers.

Children

The app is intended for pilots and is not directed at children.
